Short answers about how Rakam works.

We do not promise an outcome we cannot control. We commit to clear work, honest reporting, and taking responsibility for our mistakes. We report outcomes as they appear in your business records.

Campaign management is part of the work when it is the right step. We also look at the offer, response, follow-up, sales call, and payment, because what happens around a campaign affects what it can achieve.

We need a named person responsible for following up leads, plus the sales figures and margins we agree to use. Ad-account permissions and customer data are scoped to the work and limited to what is necessary.

We work with Arab businesses in Türkiye and with businesses looking for customers abroad. We start with the business problem and whether the work is a fit, not the size of the company.
